How do I set it up?

Install Nido on your phone and on the old iPhone. On your phone create a family and tap Add a baby station; on the old iPhone tap Use this phone as a baby station and scan the code. Both devices must be on the same Wi‑Fi.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Nido works over your home Wi‑Fi only. There are no accounts and no servers; your video and sound never leave your home.

Will I hear alerts with my phone locked?

Yes. Keep the app open in the background with the sound on, and allow notifications. Sound from the nursery keeps playing on the lock screen.

Why must the baby station stay on screen and on the charger?

iOS stops the camera when an app goes to the background, so the baby station keeps its screen on (it dims itself). Monitoring all night uses a lot of power, so keep it plugged in.

How do I change the app's language?

Open Family (or the baby station's settings) and tap Language. Pick a language, then close the app in the app switcher and open it again.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Open Family → Nido Premium and tap Restore purchases. Premium bought with Family Sharing works for everyone in your Apple family.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Nido. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Is Nido a medical device?

No. Nido helps you keep an ear and an eye on your child but does not replace adult supervision and doesn't measure breathing or other vital signs.

Nido is not a medical device and does not replace adult supervision.
