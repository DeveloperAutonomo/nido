Nido Baby Monitor

Turn an old iPhone into a baby monitor. Video, sound and alerts over your home Wi‑Fi — no cloud, no account.

Any old iPhone

Put a spare iPhone, iPad or iPod touch next to the crib. Even an iPhone 6s or the first iPhone SE works as a baby station.

Alerts that get louder

Crying, loud sounds, movement and climbing out of the crib each have their own alert. It grows louder until a parent responds.

Live video and sound

Watch and listen from any room, with the screen locked too. Turn on a night light, talk to your child or start a video call.

Every room at once

The control panel shows all baby stations side by side. Parents can also use iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Apple TV.

Sleep at a glance

Bedtime, wake-ups, the longest stretch and crying by night, week and month. Every event is saved with a short clip.

Stays at home

Video and sound go straight from phone to phone over your Wi‑Fi, encrypted. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

Free: one baby station with sound and every alert. Premium adds video, more rooms and parents, and sleep statistics.

Nido is not a medical device and does not replace adult supervision.
