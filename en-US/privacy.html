Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

The short version

Nido has no servers and no accounts. Video, sound, events, notes, sleep data and your family's names stay on your family's own devices and travel only between them over your home Wi‑Fi, encrypted. We, the developer, never receive any of it. The free version shows ads from Google, which collects some data as described below; Premium has no ads.

What stays on your devices

• Sound and video from the baby station, and short recordings of events, saved on the baby station and on the parents' devices that play them. • Family details you enter: family and parent names, children's names, birth dates, height and weight, avatars or photos you choose. • Events, notes, tags and sleep statistics. • Settings and the family's security key (in the device's Keychain). This data is sent only between devices that joined your family by scanning its QR code, directly over the local network, encrypted with TLS. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and Nido makes no backups to any cloud.

Permissions

• Microphone: to hear the room and to let a parent talk to the baby. • Camera: for the video monitor and to scan family QR codes. • Local network: to connect the family's devices to each other. • Notifications: for alerts. • Add to Photos: only when you save a recording yourself. • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): asked in the free version so ads can be more relevant; you can say no, ads still appear.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are first asked for consent. Google may collect the device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) or other device identifiers, an approximate location derived from the IP address, information about ad views and taps, and diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance. Google uses it to show, measure and limit ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partners' apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Premium removes all ads, and then no data is collected for ads.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we only learn from Apple's receipt on your device which plan is active.

Children

Nido is an app for parents and caregivers. Pictures and sounds of children stay on the family's devices. We do not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until you delete it: erase recordings on a baby station, remove a device or a child in Family, leave the family, or delete the app. Removing a device from the family changes the family's key, so it can no longer connect.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; you control everything in the app. For the data Google collects for ads, turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, use Google's controls at https://myadcenter.google.com, or get Premium, which has no ads. You can always contact us with questions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
